Guidelines & Rules

Eligible Actions

Use sustainable transport, reduce plastic waste, and conserve energy to reduce carbon footprint. Participate in eco-friendly activities related to this challenge.

Tracking Type

Your progress is automatically tracked based on your logs in the linked modules. No manual entry is required! You can manually log your progress for this challenge every day.

Evaluation

This is a Budget challenge. Your goal is to stay under the limit set by the target value. Exceeding it will fail the challenge. This is an Achievement challenge. Your goal is to reach or exceed the target value before the end date.
